## 1. Executive Summary

This inventory documents every formula, metric, indicator, heuristic score, aggregation algorithm, and prediction routine used across JalDrishti. 

The audit establishes a strict distinction among:
1. **Directly observed measurements:** Raw time-stamped readings (e.g. CGWB DWLR/manual well water level in meters below ground level).
2. **Deterministic scientific calculations:** Physical and mathematical formulas grounded in hydrology and geodesy (e.g. SPI-3 Gamma distribution, geodesic polygon excess area, area-weighted precipitation clipping, OLS temporal regression slopes).
3. **Statistical estimates:** Climatological long-period averages (1991–2020 IMD normal) and seasonal delta baselines.
4. **Machine-learning predictions:** Statistical quantile gradient boosting and autoregressive models projecting future depth horizons ($P_{10}, P_{50}, P_{90}$).
5. **Operational policy thresholds:** Administrative rules determining intervention scheme eligibility (e.g., Atal Bhujal Yojana, Amrit Sarovar).
6. **Demonstration-only values:** Static fixtures and uncalibrated heuristic scores (e.g., legacy hardcoded GSS scores) quarantined to tests.

## 3. Decision Summary

1. **Retained Deterministic Calculations (8):** Geodesic polygon area, Sutherland-Hodgman clipping, area-weighted precipitation, rainfall anomaly, SPI-3 Gamma calculation, OLS linear regression slope, arithmetic station means, and scheme eligibility matching. These are sound physical/statistical calculations with explicit units and null-handling.
2. **Retained with Scientific Safeguards (5):** Well depth normalization, rainfall deficit normalization, GRACE TWSA normalization, NDVI normalization, and Composite Descriptive GSS. Retained strictly as heuristic descriptive screening indices with explicit disclaimers; missing data produces `null` and `'gap'`, never manufactured scores.
3. **Retained as Hydrological Benchmarks (2):** Last-observation persistence and Seasonal Persistence Baseline with seasonal deltas. Used as mandatory performance gates for ML evaluation.
4. **Retained as Research Prototype (2):** Multi-output Quantile Gradient Boosting model and anchor-preserving quantile ordering. Quarantined behind strict horizon and provenance gates in `api/forecast`.
5. **Completely Disabled & Removed (1):** Synthetic companion time series formulas in the monitoring view.
